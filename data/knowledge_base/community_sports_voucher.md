# Community Sports Voucher (CSV)

## What it is
$200 in vouchers each year for youth to join community sports programmes such as swimming, football, badminton and climbing at participating community clubs.

## Who is eligible
- Aged 13 to 25.
- Singapore citizen or permanent resident.
- No income limit and no documents required.

## How to apply
Submit an application online. Vouchers are issued within 3 working days and expire on 31 December of the year of issue.
