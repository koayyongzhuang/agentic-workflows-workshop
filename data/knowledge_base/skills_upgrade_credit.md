# Skills Upgrade Credit (SUC)

## What it is
A one-off $1,000 credit that working-age residents can use to pay for approved training courses, including digital skills, data analytics, AI literacy and trade certifications.

## Who is eligible
- Aged 25 to 64.
- Singapore citizen or permanent resident.
- There is no income limit.

## Using the credit
The credit is valid for 3 years from approval. It can be split across several courses. Course fees above the credit amount are paid by the learner. Only courses on the approved course directory qualify.

## How to apply
Submit an application with a course enrolment confirmation. No appointment is required. Approval is usually given within 5 working days.
