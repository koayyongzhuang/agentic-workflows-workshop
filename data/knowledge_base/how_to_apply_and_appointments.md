# How to apply for citizen support schemes

## General steps
1. **Find a scheme.** Browse the scheme list or ask the virtual assistant which schemes match your situation.
2. **Check eligibility.** Provide age, monthly household income, household size and residency status. Per-capita income is monthly household income divided by household size.
3. **Book an appointment if required.** Only the Senior Mobility Grant needs an appointment (a home mobility assessment). Other schemes are applied for directly.
4. **Submit the application** with the required documents.
5. **Track the status** using your application ID (format APP-1234).

## Appointments
Appointments are available on weekdays at 10:00 and 15:00 at the Central, North and East Service Centres. You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the slot. Bring the applicant reference number, not identity documents, to the first visit.

## Processing times
Most applications are decided within 14 working days. Incomplete applications are returned with a list of missing documents.
