# Household Energy Rebate (HER)

## What it is
A quarterly credit applied directly to the household utilities bill to help lower- and middle-income households manage electricity and water costs.

## Rebate amounts
| Monthly per-capita household income | Rebate per quarter |
|---|---|
| $0 to $1,000 | $150 |
| $1,001 to $1,800 | $100 |
| $1,801 to $2,500 | $60 |
| above $2,500 | not eligible |

## Who is eligible
- The account holder is a Singapore citizen aged 21 or above.
- Monthly per-capita household income is $2,500 or less.
- One rebate per household, even if several members qualify.

## How to apply
No appointment is needed. Submit an application with the latest utilities bill. Credits start from the next billing quarter and renew automatically each year unless household circumstances change.

## Common questions
**We moved house.** Update the utilities account first, then resubmit; the rebate follows the account, not the person.
