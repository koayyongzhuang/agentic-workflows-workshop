# Senior Mobility Grant (SMG)

## What it covers
The Senior Mobility Grant co-pays mobility aids that help seniors stay independent at home and in the community. Covered items include wheelchairs, motorised scooters, walking frames, rollators, commodes and home grab rails. Consumables such as adult diapers are not covered.

## How much you can receive
Eligible applicants receive up to $2,000 every 5 years. The grant pays up to 90% of the item cost for households with per-capita income up to $1,000, and up to 75% for per-capita income between $1,001 and $1,500.

## Who is eligible
- Aged 60 or above.
- Singapore citizen or permanent resident (PR).
- Monthly per-capita household income of $1,500 or less. Per-capita income is total monthly household income divided by the number of people living in the household.
- Assessed by a therapist as needing the mobility aid.

## How to apply
1. Check eligibility using the eligibility checker.
2. Book a home mobility assessment. A therapist visits within 10 working days of the appointment date and recommends suitable aids.
3. Submit the application with proof of household income for the last 3 months and the vendor quotation for the recommended aid.
4. Applications are processed within 14 working days. Approved amounts are paid directly to the vendor.

## Common questions
**Can a family member apply on my behalf?** Yes, with the senior's written consent.
**I already received SMG 3 years ago.** You can apply again once 5 years have passed since the last approval, or earlier if the therapist certifies a change in condition.
