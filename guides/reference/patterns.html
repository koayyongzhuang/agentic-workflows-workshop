Multi-agent patterns

The five patterns from the slide: their shape, when to use each, and where to find them in the repo.

Names differ between sources

The slide, the repo and the readings use different names for some patterns. The trap: the slide's Orchestrator is what Anthropic calls routing, and Anthropic's orchestrator-workers is the slide's Hierarchical Decomposition.

1 · Sequential Pipeline Assembly Line

Use when
